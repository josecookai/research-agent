# RED TEAM OPENING ARGUMENT: AGI's Net Economic Impact Will Be Severely Negative

## FRAMING

The question is not whether AGI creates *some* economic value — it will. The question is whether that value accrues broadly to humanity, or narrowly to capital owners, and whether the transition destroys more than it creates in the window that matters: the next 10–20 years.

---

## 1. UNEMPLOYMENT: AGI Is Qualitatively Different From All Prior Automation

**The cognitive barrier has fallen**: Every prior wave automated physical or routine-cognitive tasks, leaving complex judgment-intensive work untouched. That was the escape valve. AGI eliminates it. A system passing bar exams, diagnosing medical images, writing production-grade software leaves no residual "human-only" cognitive domain.

**Concrete data already visible**: BLS January 2025 — professional services job openings **-20% YoY**. Dario Amodei (Anthropic CEO): AI could eliminate "half of all entry-level white-collar jobs" within 5 years.

**Scale**: McKinsey: 400–800M displaced globally by 2030. Goldman: 300M exposed. IMF: 40% globally at high exposure.

**Speed is decisive**: Industrial Revolution: ~50-year adjustment lag. Computerization: 20+ year wage stagnation. AGI arrives 2026–2029. Adoption pace ~10x faster than electrification. The historical "eventually" requires decades the displaced workers don't have.

**Skills mismatch is categorical**: A 55-year-old paralegal cannot become a prompt engineer in 2 years. New AGI-era jobs require years of foundational education to access.

---

## 2. GDP: Growth That Doesn't Reach Workers Is Not Economic Success

**Labor share already at record low 53.8%** (Q3 2025) — BEFORE AGI, trending down. AGI accelerates this to its logical conclusion.

**Acemoglu (Nobel 2024) vs. Goldman**: Acemoglu projects only **+1.1–1.6% GDP over 10 years** — 5x lower than Goldman's +7%. Core dispute: Goldman assumes 25% of tasks automatable; Acemoglu assumes 4.6%. Nobel Prize methodology > investment bank sales material.

**Historical pattern**: Computerization → widened college wage premium, flatlining wages for non-degree workers. Electrification → reduced labor's share of GDP. Every capital-biased tech revolution in modern history: GDP growth + worsening distributional outcomes.

**Gini trajectory**: Already 0.48–0.49. IMF unrest threshold: 0.40. Blanco-Grier political instability: 0.45. We are already above both thresholds. AGI pushes Gini toward 0.55–0.60.

## 3. SOCIAL STABILITY: We Are Already Above the Breaking Point

**Great Depression mechanism**: 4% → 25% unemployment → Hitler, European fascism, global authoritarianism. The mechanism: economic desperation + institutional delegitimization + authoritarian entrepreneurs.

**Rust Belt ratio**: 34% sectoral job loss over 30 years → 2016 political revolt, opioid crisis, civic collapse. McKinsey projects 57% of U.S. work hours automatable TODAY. Compressed into 5–10 years.

**Democratic erosion already underway**: Brexit, MAGA, Le Pen, Orban, Meloni, Bolsonaro — from existing inequality. We haven't seen the AGI wave yet.

**Policy response arithmetic is impossible in available window**: UBI for 50M displaced U.S. workers at $30K/year = **$1.5 trillion/year**. Entire U.S. discretionary budget: ~$1.7 trillion. Politically impossible in 2–5 year AGI window, opposed by same capital owners capturing gains.

## 5. PRE-EMPTING BLUE TEAM

**"Every tech wave created more jobs"** → True historically; irrelevant to AGI. Prior waves created manual→cognitive job ladders. AGI destroys the cognitive ladder. Speed: historical 20–50 year adjustment window vs. AGI's 2–5 years. New AI jobs are quantitatively insufficient for displaced workers.

**"GDP growth funds safety nets"** → Ignores 40 years of U.S. political economy. Labor share declined despite continuous GDP growth. Capital owners capturing gains systematically oppose redistribution — successfully. AGI concentrates political power further.

**"Institutions are stronger today"** → Directly contradicted by observable evidence. Government trust below 20% (Gallup). First peaceful transfer of power failure in 2021. European far-right at post-WWII highs simultaneously. Gini already above IMF stability threshold. We are inside the danger zone.

## PROBABILITY ASSESSMENT

**75–80% probability of significantly negative outcomes**: net job displacement exceeding creation 2026–2035, labor share declining below 50%, Gini rising above 0.52, at least one G7/G20 democratic government experiencing acute AI-linked political instability.

Remaining 20–25% contingent on: aggressive redistributive policy enacted *before* displacement peaks (2026–2028), international coordination on deployment speed, new job categories accessible without multi-year retraining. None currently on credible political trajectory.

**AGI will create the greatest transfer of economic power in human history. On current evidence, that is the most probable outcome.**
