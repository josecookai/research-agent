# AGI Economic Impact: Comprehensive Research Brief

**Prepared for Policy Debate Evidence Base | Research Date: April 2026**

## SECTION I: UNEMPLOYMENT AND LABOR MARKET DISRUPTION

### Finding 1 — The Foundational 47% Estimate (Frey & Osborne, 2013)
Oxford economists Carl Benedikt Frey and Michael Osborne published "The Future of Employment" in 2013, estimating **47% of U.S. jobs** were at high risk of automation. Transportation/logistics, office/administrative support, and production occupations ranked highest-risk.
Source: https://www.oxfordmartin.ox.ac.uk/publications/the-future-of-employment

### Finding 2 — OECD Rebuttal: Only 9% at High Risk
Arntz, Gregory, and Zierahn (2016) used a task-based approach across 21 OECD countries and found only **9% of U.S. jobs** highly automatable — one-fifth the Frey-Osborne estimate. Most occupations contain tasks that resist automation.
Source: https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/the-risk-of-automation-for-jobs-in-oecd-countries_5jlz9h56dvq7-en.html

### Finding 3 — Goldman Sachs: 300 Million Jobs at Risk from Generative AI
Goldman Sachs (Briggs & Kodnani, 2023) estimated generative AI could expose **300 million full-time jobs globally** to automation. Office/admin support: 46% of tasks automatable; Legal: 44%; Architecture/engineering: 37%; Building/maintenance: 1%.
Source: https://www.goldmansachs.com/insights/articles/generative-ai-could-raise-global-gdp-by-7-percent

### Finding 4 — McKinsey Global Institute: 400–800 Million Displaced by 2030
McKinsey (2017, updated 2024) projected **400–800 million workers** displaced globally by 2030. A November 2025 update found **57% of U.S. work hours** could be automated with today's technology. 75–375 million may need to switch occupational categories entirely.
Source: https://www.mckinsey.com/featured-insights/future-of-work/jobs-lost-jobs-gained-what-the-future-of-work-will-mean-for-jobs-skills-and-wages

### Finding 5 — WEF Future of Jobs Report 2025: Net +78 Million Jobs
WEF's January 2025 report (55 economies) projected by 2030: 170 million new roles created, 92 million displaced, **net +78 million jobs**. But 22% of all global jobs disrupted and 120+ million workers face medium-term redundancy risk.
Source: https://www.weforum.org/press/2025/01/future-of-jobs-report-2025-78-million-new-job-opportunities-by-2030-but-urgent-upskilling-needed-to-prepare-workforces/

### Finding 6 — Daron Acemoglu (MIT/Nobel 2024): Only ~5% of Economy Affected Near-Term
Acemoglu's "The Simple Macroeconomics of AI" (2024) argues AI will affect only ~**5% of the economy** near-term (office tasks involving data summary, visual matching, pattern recognition). 10-year GDP impact estimate: just **1.1–1.6% total**.
> "It's going to impact a bunch of office jobs that are about data summary, visual matching, pattern recognition — which are essentially about 5 percent of the economy." — Acemoglu
Source: https://economics.mit.edu/sites/default/files/2024-04/The%20Simple%20Macroeconomics%20of%20AI.pdf

### Finding 7 — IMF: 40% of Global Jobs Exposed; 60% in Advanced Economies
IMF (January 2024): 40% of global employment exposed; 60% of jobs in advanced economies potentially impacted. Of those exposed in advanced economies, roughly half will benefit (complementarity) and half face reduced demand (substitution).
Source: https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/publications/sdn/2024/english/sdnea2024001.pdf

### Finding 8 — BLS 2025: Software Developers +17.9%, But White-Collar Uncertainty
BLS 2025 projections (first ever incorporating AI): Software developers +17.9%; Database administrators +8.2%; Retail: only sector losing jobs. High uncertainty flagged for legal, financial, and engineering roles.
Source: https://www.bls.gov/opub/mlr/2025/article/incorporating-ai-impacts-in-bls-employment-projections.htm

### Finding 9 — White-Collar Recession 2025 Data
January 2025: lowest rate of job openings in professional services since 2013 (−20% YoY). Dario Amodei (Anthropic) projected AI could eliminate up to **half of all entry-level white-collar jobs** within 5 years, with unemployment potentially spiking **10–20%** in affected sectors.
Source: https://www.axios.com/2025/05/28/ai-jobs-white-collar-unemployment-anthropic

### Finding 10 — Historical: Industrial Revolution Adjustment Lag ~50 Years
British Industrial Revolution: real wages did not begin rising continuously until **circa 1830** — roughly 50 years after industrialization began. Incumbents did NOT move into new roles; new roles went to new labor market entrants.
Source: https://blogs.lse.ac.uk/economichistory/2022/06/01/technological-unemployment-in-victorian-britain/

### Finding 11 — Computerization (1980s–2000s): Skill-Biased Inequality
Computing revolution concentrated wage gains among college-educated workers and was the **main driver of labor market polarization from 1975 to mid-1990s**. College premium widened sharply. Declining unions also a key factor.
Source: https://economics.mit.edu/sites/default/files/publications/computing%20inequality%201998.pdf

## SECTION II: GDP AND PRODUCTIVITY IMPACT

### Finding 12 — Goldman Sachs: +7% Global GDP (~$7 Trillion) Over 10 Years
Generative AI could raise global GDP by ~7% (~$7 trillion) over a 10-year period and lift productivity growth by **1.5 percentage points annually**. Measurable U.S. GDP impact begins ~2027.
Source: https://www.goldmansachs.com/insights/articles/generative-ai-could-raise-global-gdp-by-7-percent

### Finding 13 — Acemoglu Counterestimate: Only +1.1–1.6% GDP Over 10 Years
Core dispute: Goldman assumes **25%** of work tasks automatable; Acemoglu assumes **4.6%**. This 5.5x difference in assumptions drives the 5x difference in GDP projections.
Source: https://economics.mit.edu/sites/default/files/2024-04/The%20Simple%20Macroeconomics%20of%20AI.pdf

### Finding 14 — Historical: Electricity Productivity Gains (1890–1930)
Electricity's share of U.S. manufacturing power grew from 10% (1900) to 80% (1930). Productivity gains were widely distributed only after a multi-decade adoption lag. Electrification increased wages but also reduced the labor share.
Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0014498325000014

### Finding 15 — Labor Share of U.S. GDP: Record Low 53.8% (Q3 2025)
U.S. labor share: ~70% (1947) → 62.8% (2000) → **53.8% (Q3 2025, record low**). Three-fourths of the entire post-1947 decline occurred between **2000 and 2016**.
Source: https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/LABSHPUSA156NRUG

### Finding 16 — IMF: Capital Income Always Rises Under AI; Inequality Worsens
In most IMF scenarios, **AI worsens overall inequality** through capital/labor substitution. "AI will likely worsen overall inequality, a troubling trend that policymakers must proactively address." — IMF Managing Director Georgieva, January 2024.
Source: https://www.imf.org/en/publications/fandd/issues/2024/09/ais-promise-for-the-global-economy-michael-spence

### Finding 17 — Amodei's Optimistic Scenario: 20% Annual GDP Growth Developing World
Dario Amodei ("Machines of Loving Grace", October 2024): under ideal conditions, AI could compress 50–100 years of research into 5–10 years; developing economies could see **20% annual real GDP growth**.
Source: https://www.darioamodei.com/essay/machines-of-loving-grace

## SECTION III: SOCIAL STABILITY

### Finding 18 — Great Depression Benchmark: 25% Unemployment → Democratic Collapse
U.S. unemployment: 4% (1929) → **25% (1933)**. Germany >30% unemployment → Hitler's rise to power January 1933. Majority of democracies experiencing severe Depression pushed toward authoritarianism. The 25% threshold appears historically associated with political collapse.
Source: https://www.britannica.com/event/Great-Depression

### Finding 19 — Rust Belt Deindustrialization → Political Polarization
Rust Belt lost **28% of total jobs** (1950–1980), manufacturing **−34%**. White working-class districts that voted ~80% Democratic flipped to **96% Trump** in 2016. Cascading effects: opioid crisis, abandoned communities, loss of civic institutions.
Source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/am-pdf/10.1111/1468-4446.12328

### Finding 20 — Gini Coefficient and Social Unrest: U.S. Already Above Threshold
U.S. Gini: **0.43 (1990) → 0.48–0.49 (2024)**. IMF research: Gini >0.40 associated with more social unrest. Blanco-Grier (2009): Gini >0.45 destabilizes politics. **The U.S. is already above both thresholds**, creating structural vulnerability.
Source: https://www.census.gov/library/publications/2025/demo/p60-286.html

### Finding 21 — Luddite Movement (1811–1813): First Anti-Automation Revolt
Skilled textile workers faced displacement by power looms. Context: Napoleonic Wars economic stress + technological unemployment = political violence. Government response: 13,000 troops deployed (more than Wellington had in Spain). Movement collapsed under repression; wages remained **depressed for decades**.
Source: https://www.smithsonianmag.com/history/what-the-luddites-really-fought-against-264412/

### Finding 22 — UBI Pilots: Mental Health Benefits, Mixed Employment Effects
Finland (2017–2018): €560/month → 37% reduction in depression, but no significant employment increase. Stockton SEED (2019): $500/month → recipients found full-time jobs at **double the rate** of non-recipients. Enabled seeking better employment rather than any employment.
Source: https://www.mckinsey.com/featured-insights/sustainable-inclusive-growth/chart-of-the-day/finland-sees-promising-results-after-nationwide-experiment-with-universal-basic-income

## SECTION IV: U.S. STOCK MARKET

### Finding 23 — AI Infrastructure CapEx: $600B+ in 2026 (36% YoY Increase)
Hyperscaler CapEx (Amazon, Google, Microsoft, Meta, Oracle): >$600B projected 2026 (+36% YoY). ~75% ($450B) directly tied to AI. Goldman projects $1.15 trillion total hyperscaler capex 2025–2027. Global AI startups raised $83.6B in H1 2025 alone (57.9% of all VC funding).
Source: https://www.goldmansachs.com/insights/articles/why-ai-companies-may-invest-more-than-500-billion-in-2026

### Finding 24 — Nvidia: 87% AI Chip Market Share; 112% YoY Data Center Revenue Growth
Nvidia data center revenue: $15B (2022) → $100B+ (2024) (+567% in 2 years). Q3 FY2025: $30.8B quarterly (+112% YoY). FY2026 expected: $206.4B. Peak AI chip market share: ~87%. P/E ~44x.
Source: https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-financial-results-for-third-quarter-fiscal-2025

### Finding 25 — AI Valuations vs. Dot-Com: More Rational, but Pockets of Excess
S&P Tech Index: ~30x forward earnings (elevated but vs. 55x at dot-com peak). Palantir: 407x earnings. Median AI startup: 25.8x revenue. Today's leaders are profitable revenue-generating companies, unlike dot-com era.
Source: https://www.ishares.com/us/insights/ai-stocks-bubble-2025-valuation-outlook

### Finding 26 — Alphabet Q4 2025: Cloud Revenue +48% YoY
Alphabet Q4 2025: total revenue $113.8B (+18% YoY); Cloud revenue growth accelerated from 34% to **48% YoY** in Q4 ($17.7B). Microsoft Azure: 33% revenue growth YoY.
Source: https://www.fool.com/investing/2025/11/19/nvidia-microsoft-and-google-just-changed-the-ai-ma/

## SECTION V: AGI TIMELINES

### Finding 27 — Metaculus Prediction Market: Median AGI ~2033
Metaculus (Feb 2026): 25% probability of AGI by **2029**; 50% probability (median) by **2033**. Interquartile range: ~2027–2039.
Source: https://www.metaculus.com/questions/17124/conditional-date-of-artificial-general-intelligence/

### Finding 28 — Sam Altman / Anthropic: AGI in 2026–2029
Sam Altman (January 2025): "We are now confident we know how to build AGI." Likely "during Trump's term" (before 2029). Anthropic's OSTP submission (March 2025): "We expect powerful AI systems will emerge in late 2026 or early 2027."
Source: https://time.com/7205596/sam-altman-superintelligence-agi/

### Finding 29 — Geoffrey Hinton: 50% Probability of Superintelligence Within 20 Years
Hinton (2024 Nobel): estimates AI could surpass human intelligence within 5–20 years; assigns 50% probability within 20 years. Has expressed existential risk concerns.

### Finding 30 — Epoch AI: Compute Grows 4–5x Per Year; 2e29 FLOP Feasible by 2030
Training compute has grown at **4.4x/year** (2010–2024). Scaling feasible to 2e29 FLOP by 2030 if energy/chip constraints addressed. Frontier model training costs ~$100s of millions, growing at 2.4x/year.
Source: https://epoch.ai/blog/training-compute-of-frontier-ai-models-grows-by-4-5x-per-year/
